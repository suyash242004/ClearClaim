## 💰 Why These Prices?

### Market Benchmarks (India 2026):
- **Individual (25-35 years)**: ₹7,000-₹15,000 for ₹5-10L
- **Family floater (4 members)**: ₹18,000-₹35,000 for ₹10-15L
- **Senior citizens (60+)**: ₹40,000-₹80,000 for ₹10-15L
- **Large families (8 members)**: ₹25,000-₹45,000 for ₹15-25L

### Why Parent Plans Cost 5-7x More:
1. **Age-related risk**: 60+ age group has 6-8x higher hospitalization rate
2. **Pre-existing diseases**: 80% of seniors have diabetes, hypertension, or arthritis
3. **Longer hospital stays**: Average 7-10 days vs. 3-4 days for younger patients
4. **Co-payment mandatory**: Most parent plans require 20-30% co-pay
5. **Higher claim frequency**: Seniors file 3-4 claims vs. 0.5-1 for young adults

### Medical Inflation in India:
- **14% per year** (highest in Asia!)
- A surgery costing ₹1.5L in 2020 → ₹4L+ in 2026
- ICU costs: ₹15,000-50,000 per day in metro cities
- Critical illness (cancer, heart): ₹20-40L total treatment cost

## 🔐 Real-World Business Rules

### 1. **Waiting Periods**

| Type | Duration | What's Not Covered |
|------|----------|-------------------|
| **Initial** | 30 days | All non-accidental claims |
| **Pre-Existing Diseases** | 2-4 years | Diabetes, hypertension, asthma, etc. |
| **Specific Diseases** | 2 years | Hernia, cataracts, joint replacement, kidney stones |
| **Maternity** | 9-24 months | Pregnancy, childbirth, newborn care |

**Exception**: Accidental claims covered from Day 1.

### 2. **What's Covered** ✅

- ✅ Hospitalization (room rent, ICU, surgery, medicines)
- ✅ Pre-hospitalization (30-60 days before admission)
- ✅ Post-hospitalization (60-90 days after discharge)
- ✅ Daycare procedures (586+ treatments not requiring 24-hour stay)
  - Chemotherapy, dialysis, cataract surgery, lithotripsy
- ✅ Ambulance charges

  - Air: ₹25,000-50,000 (critical cases)

- ✅ Modern treatments (robotic surgery, PET scan, proton therapy)

- ✅ Mental health treatment (new in 2025-26 plans)

### 4. **Co-Payment Requirements**

Co-payment = Customer pays X% of claim, insurance pays rest.

| Plan Type | Co-Pay % | Why? |
|-----------|----------|------|
| Personal/Family | 0-10% | Optional, reduces premium by 10-20% |
| Parent Plan | 20-30% | **Mandatory** - reduces moral hazard |
| Senior Gold | 0% | Premium feature - full cashless |
| Zone-based | +10% | If treatment in city outside policy zone |

**Example**: ₹1,00,000 claim with 20% co-pay → Customer pays ₹20,000, insurer pays ₹80,000.

### 6. **Premium Calculation Factors**

Base premium is adjusted by:

| Factor | Impact | Example |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **Age** | +15% every 5 years after 40 | 25-year-old: ₹10K, 45-year-old: ₹23K |
| **Zone** | Metro +10-15% | Delhi/Mumbai cost more than Pune/Jaipur |
| **Medical History** | Pre-existing +20-30% | Diabetes adds ₹3-5K to premium |
| **Family Size** | 4+ members: -10-15% | Bulk discount for larger families |
| **Policy Tenure** | 2-year: -10%, 3-year: -15% | Discount for multi-year commitment |
| **No-Claim Bonus (NCB)** | +5-50% cumulative | Claim-free year → +10% coverage OR -10% premium |
| **Lifestyle** | Smoker: +20%, Gym: -5% | Healthy habits rewarded |

### 7. **Claim Settlement Process**

#### Cashless Claims (Network Hospitals):
1. **Pre-Authorization**: Submit request 48 hours before planned admission
2. **Hospital Verification**: Insurance TPA verifies policy validity
3. **Treatment**: Hospital bills insurer directly
4. **Discharge**: Pay only co-pay (if applicable) + non-medical expenses

#### Reimbursement Claims (Non-Network):
1. **Treatment**: Pay hospital bills yourself
2. **Submit Documents**: Discharge summary, bills, prescriptions, reports
3. **Processing**: 15-22 days (without AI - **instant with ClearClaim!**)
4. **Payment**: Direct bank transfer

**Required Documents**:
- Discharge summary
- Original bills & receipts
- Prescriptions
- Diagnostic reports (X-ray, MRI, blood tests)
- Doctor's consultation notes
- Policy copy
